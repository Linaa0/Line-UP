its variants and where it will live in code:

- Navigation (bottom tab bar, back header) → `frontend/src/components/navigation/`
- Cross-feature composites (empty state, route badge) → `frontend/src/components/shared/`
- Anything used by one feature only → `frontend/src/features/<feature>/components/`
